The Cherry on Top (The "Holy Sh*t" Feature)

If you only add one thing beyond the roadmap, make it this:

Workout Replay & Error Timeline

At the end of a session, the user gets:

A timeline of every rep.
Click on Rep 6 → jumps to that exact moment in the recorded video.
Overlay highlights the knees in red and displays: "Knees moved 9 cm beyond toes for 0.42 s."
Side panel shows metrics for that rep (depth, tempo, symmetry, score).
Export the session as a PDF report.

This combines computer vision, analytics, software engineering, and polished UX into one feature. It's memorable, easy to demo in interviews, and much harder to dismiss as "just another MediaPipe project."
